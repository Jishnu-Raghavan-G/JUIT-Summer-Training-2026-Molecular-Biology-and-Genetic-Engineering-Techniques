# References

This section contains the main academic and institutional sources used for preparing the JUIT Summer Training documentation.

The references were used to verify concepts, terminology, experimental principles, laboratory practices, molecular biology techniques, and animal-handling guidelines.

## Molecular Biology and Biochemistry

1. Alberts, B. et al. *Molecular Biology of the Cell*. Garland Science.

2. Lodish, H. et al. *Molecular Cell Biology*. W. H. Freeman.

3. Nelson, D. L. and Cox, M. M. *Lehninger Principles of Biochemistry*. W. H. Freeman.

4. Berg, J. M., Tymoczko, J. L., Gatto, G. J. and Stryer, L. *Biochemistry*. W. H. Freeman.

## Molecular Biology Techniques

5. Green, M. R. and Sambrook, J. *Molecular Cloning: A Laboratory Manual*. Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory Press.

6. Wilson, K. and Walker, J. *Principles and Techniques of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology*. Cambridge University Press.

7. Brown, T. A. *Gene Cloning and DNA Analysis: An Introduction*. Wiley-Blackwell.

## PCR and DNA Analysis

9. Saiki, R. K. et al. Primer-directed enzymatic amplification of DNA with a thermostable DNA polymerase. *Science*.

10. Sambrook, J. and Russell, D. W. *Molecular Cloning: A Laboratory Manual*. Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory Press.

## Microbiology and Molecular Identification

14. Clarridge, J. E. Impact of 16S rRNA gene sequence analysis for identification of bacteria on clinical microbiology and infectious diseases. *Clinical Microbiology Reviews*.

## Laboratory Animal Science and Ethics

18. National Research Council. *Guide for the Care and Use of Laboratory Animals*. National Academies Press.

## General Laboratory Practice

21. World Health Organization (WHO). *Laboratory Biosafety Manual*.

22. National Institutes of Health (NIH). Laboratory safety and biosafety resources.

23. Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA). Laboratory safety guidance.

## Note on Sources

The references above were used as supporting academic and institutional resources. The notes in this repository are written as a personal learning record rather than as reproductions of these sources.

Experimental procedures should always be performed according to the specific protocol, equipment instructions, laboratory SOPs, biosafety requirements, and institutional supervision applicable to the experiment.

For animal experiments, approved institutional protocols and applicable ethical guidelines must take priority over general descriptions in these notes.

## Repository Scope

The repository documents the major concepts and techniques covered during the Summer Training Programme, including:

- Molecular biology fundamentals
- Laboratory techniques
- DNA isolation and analysis
- Restriction digestion and DNA ligation
- PCR and gene amplification
- Recombinant DNA technology
- Protein extraction and analysis
- Molecular methods for microbial identification
- Laboratory animal handling
- Animal safety and ethics

The purpose of this repository is to preserve the knowledge, laboratory concepts, and practical learning gained during the training programme in an organized and reproducible form.
